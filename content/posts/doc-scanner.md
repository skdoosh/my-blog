# Document Scanner Demo

Upload an image of a document (or take a photo directly from your phone camera) and watch it get automatically scanned and enhanced!

## How It Works

This scanner uses computer vision to:
1. Detect document edges
2. Apply perspective correction
3. Enhance text readability

Try it out with a photo of a receipt, business card, or any document!
